# Acme Pipeline: product sheet (sample material for the sales bench)

Acme Pipeline is a sales engagement platform: email sequences, a dialer, and
call recording with notes logged to the CRM.

## Plans and pricing
- Team: $45 per seat per month, billed monthly or annually.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, minimum 25 seats.
- Annual billing: two months free (you pay for 10 months).
- Free trial: 14 days, up to 5 seats, no credit card.

## Integrations
- Salesforce and HubSpot: two-way sync of contacts, activities and deals.
- Gmail and Outlook for email sequences; Slack for notifications.
- There is no Pipedrive integration today.

## Security
- SOC 2 Type II report, shared under NDA.
- Single sign-on (SAML) on the Enterprise plan only.
- Data hosted on AWS in us-east-1 or eu-west-1; the customer picks the region at signup.

## Onboarding and support
- Team plan: self-serve setup.
- Enterprise: a named onboarding manager; most teams are live in 2 to 3 weeks.
- Support: email on Team; Enterprise adds phone support with a 4-hour response on business days.
