Key phrase: in a bullet, you can put the most important phrase between double asterisks, like **Go Live** or **one session at a time**. Choose the direct answer, a number, a price, a name, a limit or the exact label of a button or menu. Mark at most one phrase per bullet and at most three per answer, and use five words or fewer for each. Leave a bullet without a mark when nothing in it stands out. This mark is the only formatting you can use, and it overrides any rule above against bold.
