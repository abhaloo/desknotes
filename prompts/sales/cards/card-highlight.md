Key phrases: in each line, put the phrase that carries that line's main point between double asterisks, in five words or fewer, like **per seat** or **runs next to your recorder**. Mark the topic of the line, not filler words. Never mark the label. Mark at most one phrase per line, and leave a line unmarked when nothing in it stands out. This mark is the only formatting you can use, and it overrides any rule above against bold.
